  • recall that: 0 × 9 = 0, 1 × 9 = 9, 2 × 9 = 18, 3 × 9 = 27, 4 × 9 = 36, 5 × 9 = 45, 6 × 9 = 54, 7 × 9 = 63, 8 × 9 = 72, 9 × 9 = 81
  • this list of single-digit numbers times the divisor is here called a "chunk table"
  • the dividend is a minuend.
  • 12 times 10 million minus 9 times 10 million is 3 times 10 million just like how 12 times 1 minus 9 times 1 is 3 times 1. 1 is a power of 10
  • We subtract a digit of the quotient times the divisor times a power of 10 from the dividend
  • we keep the result and "carry down" that is, preserve the digits unaffected by the subtraction, and then subtract the next digit times the divisor times a power of 10
