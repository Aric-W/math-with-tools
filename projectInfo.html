why was this site made

aside from my lifelong interest in the subject matter, I made this to show users that they can be their own calculators and thereby build

  • confidence: arithmetic can be conceived as a physical process, in this case moving beads. observing this will build confidence in the user's possibility of doing arithmetic by hand.
  • independence: users will realize their potential independence from calculators.

how much ai was used in the creation of this website?

LLMs were used extensively to write and rewrite HTML and JavaScript code

I used both the pro and fast models of Google Gemini, I just wrote prompts into the browser chat.

I decided I couldn't not use generative ai after seeing the progress made in mathematics by various models

furthermore I believe that by their very nature LLMS are better than humans at using the tedious syntax of things like programming languages and mathematics

It's difficult to say what was ai generated and what was human made in the html and most of the JavaScript files since I frequently used suggestions and had it rewrite code

I was able to keep straight what was NOT generated by artificial intelligence:

  • nothing in dataModel.js
  • all of the assets were human-made by me using GIMP, the snipping tool and Microsoft Paint
  • all photos were taken using an iPhone 14 Pro camera
  • I designed the page layouts
  • the ideas presented here are all original to me but I was inspired by other human composed texts online
